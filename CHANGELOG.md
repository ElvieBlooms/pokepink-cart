# Changelog

All notable changes to this cart are recorded here, newest first.

## [0.1.0-beta.2] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Name Tags 1.3.0, which nicknames the Pokémon you catch and gives trainers'
  Pokémon their canon nicknames.

### Changed

- The Pink mods are pinned at 0.3.1: Pink's gym leaders keep their colours
  with ADVANCED colours on, and catching a Pikachu unlocks the new Not
  Version Exclusive achievement (77 achievements, 567 points).

## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- First public beta of Pokémon Pink: the eleven Pink mods at 0.3.0,
  Recomp Achievements 2.8.0, Facets 1.2.4 and Emerald Lens 1.1.1, with
  starting settings for Facets, Emerald Lens and Recomp Achievements.
- The cart label from the Pokémon Pink project.
