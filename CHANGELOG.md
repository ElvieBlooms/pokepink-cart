# Changelog

All notable changes to this cart are recorded here, newest first.

## [0.1.0-beta.3] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- Recomp Achievements Online 2.9.1, Field Notes 0.2.0 and Catch of the Day
  0.1.0-beta.4, installed but switched off. Turn them on in the Mods manager
  if you want the online leaderboard, the tester's checklists or daily
  swarms and contests.

### Changed

- The Pink mods are pinned at 0.3.2: wild Pikachu from other mods, such as
  a Catch of the Day swarm, are real Pikachu instead of Clefairy, and a
  Clefairy from another mod is a Clefairy.
- Recomp Achievements 2.9.1 (shares the second screen with Field Notes),
  Facets 1.3.3, Emerald Lens 1.2.0, Name Tags 1.4.0 and PRISM Importer 0.4.0.
- `tools/repin.py` can add a second mod from one repo, ship a pin switched
  off, and falls back to release tags when GitHub's release page is out of
  reach.

## [0.1.0-beta.2] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Name Tags 1.3.0, which nicknames the Pokémon you catch and gives trainers'
  Pokémon their canon nicknames.

### Changed

- The Pink mods are pinned at 0.3.1: Pink's gym leaders keep their colours
  with ADVANCED colours on, and catching a Pikachu unlocks the new Not
  Version Exclusive achievement (77 achievements, 567 points).

## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- First public beta of Pokémon Pink: the eleven Pink mods at 0.3.0,
  Recomp Achievements 2.8.0, Facets 1.2.4 and Emerald Lens 1.1.1, with
  starting settings for Facets, Emerald Lens and Recomp Achievements.
- The cart label from the Pokémon Pink project.
